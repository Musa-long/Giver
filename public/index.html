Frequently asked

Everything about the giveaway.

Is this really free?

Yes. The giveaway itself costs nothing. You only pay normal network gas to move assets, which is why a $5 minimum balance is required.

Which networks are supported?

Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Avalanche, Linea, Scroll, zkSync Era, Mantle, Blast, Gnosis, Celo, Cronos, Polygon zkEVM and Tron.

Why does my wallet ask for several confirmations?

Each network needs its own approval. Confirm each prompt in order — the process is fully automatic once started.

How is the $1,000 distributed?

Equally across the eligible wallets recorded in the round, paid in USDT after the campaign closes.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Tap Connect Wallet and choose WalletConnect, then scan the QR with any mobile wallet — MetaMask, Trust, Rainbow, OKX and 400+ others. For Tron, open this page in the TronLink browser.
